Most people making content get stuck on one question: how do you actually make money? There are really only three paths, and which one fits depends on whether you have **traffic first, or skill first**.

## 1. Ad revenue: traffic first, money later

A platform shares ad income with you based on views or reads — creator funds, a blog running Google AdSense, and so on.

- **The bar:** you need to **build an audience first.** Without volume, the payout is pennies.
- **Best for:** people who can publish consistently and whose content has genuine search or recommendation value.
- **The catch:** low rates, and you're at the mercy of platform rules — one algorithm change can halve your income.

> A steadier version is **running your own content site with ads** — the traffic and content are yours, not the platform's. It still takes time to build.

## 2. Affiliate commissions: trust converts

Recommend products, drop a link, earn a commission on sales.

- **The bar:** lower than ads — **a few thousand targeted followers is enough to start.**
- **Best for:** niche accounts with high follower trust (parenting, tech, beauty).
- **The catch:** product selection is everything. Push something bad once and you lose trust; the high-commission items are often the hardest to sell.

## 3. Selling services or knowledge: skill, not scale

Take on work, consult, sell courses, templates, or a community. This path **doesn't need big traffic — it needs real ability.**

- **The bar:** you genuinely have to be better than most at something.
- **Best for:** people with a craft (design, editing, writing, ops, coding).
- **The catch:** it's easy to trap yourself trading time for money. The fix is to **productize** the service — turn it into a course, a template, a tool.

## How to choose

- **Have skill, no traffic** → start by selling services, build a reputation through delivery, and make content on the side.
- **Can publish consistently, no way to monetize yet** → start with ads and affiliates, and accumulate.

Don't aim to "go big first, then monetize." **Close one minimal money loop first** — even $70 a month beats a hundred thousand idle followers.
